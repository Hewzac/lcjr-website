Our Story

Two kids. One truck. A valley that needed a hauler who shows up.

LCJR was started on March 30th by two 17-year-olds with a truck, a work ethic, and a simple idea.

We wanted to bring a junk removal service to the Lewis-Clark Valley, all while maintaining speed, affordability, and most importantly, reliability. Every job gets the same respect: show up on time, work hard, be fair, and always leave the site cleaner than we found it.

What We Hold To

Four rules on every job.

  1. 01

    Show up on time

    A window we give you is a window we keep. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

  2. 02

    Work hard

    You point, we lift. Stairs, basements and awkward corners are part of the job.

  3. 03

    Be fair

    One flat price before we start. No mid-job renegotiating, no surprise fees.

  4. 04

    Leave it cleaner

    We sweep up before we pull out. The space should look better than we found it.
