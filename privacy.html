1. Who we are

Lewis-Clark Junk Removal ("LCJR", "we", "us") is a junk removal business operating in the Lewis-Clark Valley, legally organized as [BUSINESS LEGAL NAME]. This policy covers the website at lcjr.com and the information you send us when you ask for a quote or book a job.

2. What we collect

What you type into the quote form

The form on our quote page asks for exactly this, and nothing more:

  • Your name and phone number — required, because we have to be able to reach you with a price.
  • Email address — optional. Leave it blank if you'd rather we only call or text.
  • Job type — residential pickup, estate cleanout, construction debris, yard waste, or a business account.
  • City — Lewiston, Clarkston, Asotin, or somewhere else.
  • What needs hauling — a free-text description. Whatever you choose to put here comes to us, so only include what you want us to have.
  • Timing — how soon you need it gone.

We do not ask for, and have no use for, your date of birth, Social Security number, driver's license, or any financial account details. If a form or a caller ever asks you for those while claiming to be us, it isn't us.

What you tell us another way

Most of our work starts with a phone call, a text, or an Instagram message. When it does, we end up with whatever you sent: your phone number, the content of your messages, and any photos of the pile or the property you send to help us price the job.

What we need to actually do the work

Once you book, we need the service address and access details — a gate code, where the items are, which door to use. We collect that at booking, not from the website.

What the website itself records

Our web host keeps standard server logs, which typically include IP addresses, the pages requested, timestamps, and browser type. That happens automatically for any website; we don't use those logs to build a profile of you.

3. How we use it

We use what you send for the job and nothing else. Specifically:

  • To work out a price and get back to you with it.
  • To schedule the pickup and find the property.
  • To contact you about that job — running late, a question about an item, a confirmation when it's done.
  • To invoice you, and for commercial accounts, to keep per-visit records.
  • To keep the records a business is required to keep, such as for taxes.

We do not sell your information. We don't rent, trade or share it with data brokers, lead-generation services, or advertisers. We won't add you to a marketing list because you asked for a quote.

4. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording, and no third-party chat widget. There is nothing here following you between sites, and nothing to opt out of.

One thing worth naming plainly: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and basic request information, the same as any file your browser fetches from another server. Google's handling of that is governed by their own privacy policy, not ours. If that matters to you, the fonts can be self-hosted instead — tell us and we'll do it.

5. Who else sees it

Your information stays with the people who need it to get your junk hauled:

  • Our crew. Whoever is running your job sees your name, address, phone number and what needs moving.
  • [FORM PROVIDER]. Quote form submissions pass through a third-party form service on their way to our inbox, and are stored there under their privacy terms.
  • Ordinary service providers. Our web host, our email and phone providers, and — for commercial accounts — our bookkeeping and invoicing tools. They handle the data to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it. If we receive a valid legal demand, or we need to establish or defend a legal claim, we may have to disclose information.

If the business is ever sold or merged, customer records could transfer as part of it. The buyer would be bound by this policy for information collected under it.

6. Job photos and social media

We take before-and-after photos of our work, and we post some of them to Instagram. We stick to the pile and the space — we don't publish your name, your address, or anything identifying, and we don't photograph the inside of your home beyond what's being removed.

If you'd rather we didn't photograph your job at all, just say so. Tell the crew or mention it when you book, and that's the end of it. If something of yours is already posted and you want it taken down, email us and we'll remove it.

7. How long we keep it

Quote requests that never turn into a job get cleared out once they're clearly stale. Records for completed jobs — invoices, what we hauled, what you paid — we keep as long as tax and accounting rules require. Text and email threads stay in the phone and mailbox they arrived in until they're deleted in the normal course.

8. Your choices

No matter what state you're in, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we have. We'll describe the information we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Wrong phone number, misspelled name — tell us and we'll fix it.
  • Delete it. We'll delete what we're not legally required to keep. Records tied to a completed, paid job may have to stay for tax purposes.
  • Stop contacting you. Reply STOP to a text, or tell us directly.

Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] or call (208) 111-2222 and we'll take care of it. We may need to confirm you're the person the information belongs to before we act. There's no charge, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights beyond these. If you believe you have one we haven't honored, contact us and we'll sort it out.

9. How we protect it

This site is served over HTTPS, so what you type into the quote form is encrypted in transit. Access to the inbox and phone that receive your requests is limited to people who need it.

We're a small local business, not a bank, and we'd rather be straight with you than make promises we can't back: no system is perfectly secure. That's part of why we don't ask for financial or government ID information in the first place — the safest data is the data nobody collected.

10. Children

Our services are for adults arranging work on a property. This site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us something, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information — adding analytics, say, or a new booking tool — we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. Material changes won't be applied retroactively to information already collected without telling you.
