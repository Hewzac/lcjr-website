Questions

Before you call.

Is there a contract or a minimum?

No lock-in. A recurring slot is an arrangement, not a contract. Change the cadence or pause it whenever your workload shifts.

How is commercial pricing set?

By volume, the same as residential, but agreed up front as a standing rate so you can budget for it instead of re-quoting every visit.

How is it different from residential?

Same trucks and the same crew. A commercial account adds a written standing rate, invoicing with net terms, a named contact and per-visit records for your files.

Can you work around our hours?

Yes. Early mornings, after close or between shifts. Tell us the window that doesn't disrupt your customers and we'll work in it.

Do you handle multiple properties?

Yes. Multiple sites can run on one schedule and land on a single invoice.

What can't you take?

Hazardous material: paint, chemicals, solvents, asbestos and similar. Send a photo if you're unsure and we'll give you a straight answer.

Let's build your route together.

Tell us about your sites and how often the pile builds up. We'll come back with a written rate and a schedule, and then earn the work every visit.
