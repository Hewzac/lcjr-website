What we take. What we can't.

Not sure about something? Text a photo to (208) 503-6307 and we'll tell you straight.

We take

  • Furniture, couches and beds
  • Mattresses and box springs*
  • Appliances, including fridges and freezers*
  • TVs, monitors and electronics*
  • Construction and remodel debris
  • Yard waste, branches and brush
  • Tires*
  • Garage, shed and estate contents

* Carries a disposal fee from the facility. We tell you before we load it, never after.

We can't take

  • Paint, solvents, chemicals, fuel, oil and other hazardous liquids
  • Asbestos, or anything that may contain it
  • Ammunition, explosives, and flammable or pressurized containers
  • Medical or biological waste, including sharps
  • Human or animal remains

If we find something on this list once we're there, we leave it and take the rest.

Where it goes

  1. Donate. If it's still useful, it goes to someone who can use it.
  2. Recycle. What a facility will take back gets recycled, not buried.
  3. Dispose. The rest goes to a licensed facility, with the scale ticket to show for it.

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Pricing goes by how much room your load takes up. Not weight, not hourly. The estimate tool gives you a ballpark in a couple of minutes. A supervisor confirms one flat price on site before we start, and it doesn't change while we're loading. A few items, like mattresses, tires and fridges, carry a disposal fee. We tell you before we load them.

Do I have to be there?

Not necessarily. If everything is outside, in a garage, or otherwise easy to find, we can handle the pickup and send photos when the job is done.

How fast can you get here?

We aim to get most jobs in the valley done within 24 hours, and same-day pickups happen often. Call and ask.

What can't you take?

Hazardous material is the main one: paint, chemicals, solvents, fuel, asbestos and similar. The full list is above. If you're not sure about something, send a photo and we'll tell you straight.

What happens to my stuff?

We sort as we load. Anything still useful gets donated, what can be recycled gets recycled, and the rest goes to a licensed disposal facility.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Recurring pickups, job-site cleanups and unit turnovers, all invoiced rather than cash at the curb. See how commercial accounts work, or call (208) 503-6307 and we'll set one up.
