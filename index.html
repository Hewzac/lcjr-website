Good To Know

How much does it cost?

Pricing is based on how much space your load takes up in the truck, not on weight or hourly rates. You get one flat number before we start, and it doesn't change once we're loading.

Do I have to be there?

Not necessarily. If everything is outside, in a garage, or otherwise easy to find, we can handle the pickup and send photos when the job is done.

How fast can you get here?

Most jobs in the valley get handled within 24 hours. Same-day pickups happen often — call and ask.

What can't you take?

Hazardous material is the main one — paint, chemicals, solvents, asbestos and similar. If you're not sure about something, send a photo and we'll tell you straight.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Recurring pickups, job-site cleanups and unit turnovers all get handled on invoice rather than cash at the curb. Call or text (208) 503-6307 and we'll set up an account.
