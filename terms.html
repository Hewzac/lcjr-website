By booking work with us you're confirming you're at least 18, and that you either own the property and items involved or have the owner's permission to have them removed.

2. What we do

We haul junk: furniture and appliances, construction debris, estate and garage cleanouts, yard and green waste, and similar work for homes and businesses in the Lewis-Clark Valley and the surrounding area.

We are not movers, demolition contractors, cleaners, or a hazardous waste service. We load, remove and dispose of material. We don't perform demolition, disconnect gas or electrical appliances, or deep-clean a space beyond sweeping up after ourselves.

3. Quotes and pricing

How the price is set

Pricing is based on how much room your load takes up in the truck — not weight, not hourly. You get one flat number before we start work, and that number doesn't change while we're loading.

The online estimate tool is not a quote

The calculator on our quote page works from what you tell it — the load fraction you pick and the items you list. It produces an estimate, and we label it that way on every screen because the distinction matters.

The same goes for a figure given over the phone, by text, or from photos. All of them rest on your description of the job. The binding number is the one a supervisor gives you on site, once we can see the actual load, and always before any work begins. Most of the time it matches the estimate. When it doesn't, you get the real number first and you're free to decline.

When a price can change

If what's actually there is materially different from what was described — more volume, items we were not told about, or conditions that make the job substantially harder — we'll stop, tell you, and give you a revised price. You can accept it or decline and owe us nothing. We will never quietly add charges after the fact.

Certain items carry disposal fees charged by the facility that accepts them — things like mattresses, tires, appliances containing refrigerant, and electronics. Where those apply we'll tell you before loading, not after.

4. Scheduling and access

We aim for 24-hour turnaround on most valley jobs and we'll give you an arrival window. Weather, traffic, landfill hours, and jobs that run long are real, so a window is our best commitment, not a guarantee. If we're going to miss it, you'll hear it from us.

You're responsible for making sure we can actually get to the items — unlocked gates, a place to park the truck, and a clear path. If we arrive and can't access the job, or nobody is there to confirm what's going, we may have to reschedule.

You don't have to be present. If everything is outside, in a garage, or otherwise easy to identify, we can do the pickup and send photos when it's done — but you're responsible for clearly identifying what goes and what stays.

5. Changes and cancellations

Plans change. Cancel or reschedule by calling or texting (208) 111-2222 — we just ask for as much notice as you can reasonably give. We don't charge a cancellation fee for residential jobs. If you paid a deposit under section 8 and cancel before we start, it comes back to you.

We may cancel or reschedule if conditions are unsafe, if the job turns out to be outside what we do, or if the load includes material we're not permitted to take.

6. What we can't take

Some material we're legally not allowed to haul, and some is simply unsafe. We cannot accept:

  • Paint, solvents, chemicals, fuel, oil, and other hazardous liquids
  • Asbestos and any material that may contain it
  • Ammunition, explosives, and flammable or pressurized containers
  • Medical or biological waste, including sharps
  • Human or animal remains
  • Anything requiring licensed hazardous-waste handling or environmental remediation

If you're not sure about something, send a photo before we come out and we'll give you a straight answer. If we find prohibited material once we're there, we'll leave it and take the rest.

You agree not to conceal prohibited material in a load. If something we were not told about causes injury, property damage, or a disposal penalty, you're responsible for the resulting costs.

7. Your property and what happens to it

Check the pile before we load it. Once we remove an item at your direction, it's gone — we may recycle it, donate it, or take it to a disposal facility, often the same day. We can't retrieve items after removal and we're not responsible for anything removed as instructed.

Title to everything we haul transfers to us at the moment it's loaded, and we decide how it's disposed of. We recycle and donate what we reasonably can.

Please pull out anything valuable, sentimental, or containing personal information before we arrive. That applies especially to estate and garage cleanouts, where documents, photographs and small valuables turn up mixed into the pile. We're not able to sort or inspect the contents of what we remove.

8. Payment

For residential work, payment is due when the job is finished, unless we've agreed otherwise in writing. We accept the payment methods listed at the time of booking.

Deposits

On larger jobs — currently those priced at $400 or more — we take a 25% deposit to hold the slot. Three things about that:

  • It is taken by a supervisor after they have seen the job and set the real price. Never before.
  • It is never collected through this website. There is no card field anywhere on lcjr.com, and the estimate tool takes no payment of any kind.
  • It comes off the final bill, and it is refunded if you cancel before we start work.

Smaller residential jobs don't require a deposit. Unpaid balances may accrue reasonable late charges to the extent the law allows, and you'd be responsible for reasonable collection costs.

We'll take responsibility for damage to your property caused by our negligence. Report it to us within [N] days of the job so we can look at it while the facts are fresh.
